# Code of Conduct

We want ZETA Pro to be a welcoming project.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive, including when you disagree.
- Assume good faith; give and accept feedback graciously.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Spam, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Requests for, or sharing of, content that violates Roblox's Terms of Use or applicable law.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may restrict participation for anyone who violates these rules. Report problems to the maintainers by opening a private security/conduct report on the repository or contacting the ORG owners.

This policy is a short, original summary inspired by common open-source community standards.
